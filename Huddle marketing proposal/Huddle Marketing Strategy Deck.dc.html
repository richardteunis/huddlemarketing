01Executive Summary

The opportunity is larger than improving marketing. It's to define what the Huddle brand means.

Growth has been driven by relationships, referrals, reputation and the quality of the work. Those strengths remain fundamental — but they're no longer sufficient on their own to build the next stage of Huddle.

The next stage requires a marketing system.

02The Strategic Ambition

Become one of the most recognizable independent experience agencies in our markets.

Not the largest. Not the loudest. The most distinctive, trusted and interesting. Huddle shouldn't try to look larger than it is — it should make its size the advantage.

Closer relationships

Senior involvement, fewer layers between decision and delivery.

Faster decisions

Creative flexibility and the freedom to move at the speed of the brief.

More humanity

A partner who acts like a person, not a process.
