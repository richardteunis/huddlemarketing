01 / 12 Executive Summary

Huddle has an opportunity larger than improving its marketing. It has the opportunity to define what the Huddle brand means in the market.

Historically, growth has been driven by relationships, referrals, reputation and the quality of the work. Those strengths remain fundamental — but they are not sufficient on their own to build the next stage of Huddle.

The next stage requires a marketing system.

02 / 12 The Strategic Ambition

Become one of the most recognizable independent experience agencies in the markets we choose to serve.

Not necessarily the largest. Not necessarily the loudest. The most distinctive, trusted and interesting. Huddle should not try to look larger than it is — it should make its size the advantage.

Closer relationships

Senior-level involvement and fewer layers between decision and delivery.

Faster decisions

Greater creative flexibility and the freedom to move at the speed of the brief.

More humanity

Greater accountability and a partner who acts like a person, not a process.

03 / 12 Marketing Philosophy

Six principles behind everything we publish.

These are the operating beliefs of Huddle's marketing — the standard every initiative is held to, not slogans for a wall.

Principle 01
Be remarkable, not louder

Maximum relevance within the audience that matters beats maximum reach. A small audience that notices, remembers and recommends is worth more than a large passive following.

Principle 02
Own a point of view

"Creative, strategic, full-service" is category language, not differentiation. Huddle should be associated with a belief: experiences should feel human — and everything else exists to serve that.

Principle 03
Build memory before demand

Most buyers aren't purchasing right now. Marketing captures the demand that exists and creates the demand that hasn't arrived yet — so Huddle is already an answer when the question comes.

Principle 04
Teach before selling

Huddle's knowledge of experience design, production and audience behaviour mostly lives in people's heads. Turn it into an asset so the market thinks: these people understand where experiences are going.

Principle 05
Build a flywheel, not campaigns

Marketing shouldn't be disconnected bursts of activity. The system should compound — every client engagement becomes both revenue and future marketing capital.

Principle 06
Marketing must be measurable

Creativity without measurement is subjective; measurement without creativity optimizes mediocrity. Huddle needs both — connecting source to engagement to relationship to revenue.
